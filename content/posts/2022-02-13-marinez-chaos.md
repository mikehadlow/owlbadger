Rude, opinonated, snarky, grumpy, memoir of the authors four years or so of first creating and selling a stratup, then selling to Twitter _and_ himself to Facebook, followed by his experiences of working inside the social networking beast. Lots of great insights about silicon valley and the various forms of life that inhabit it. A really fun, enjoyable read. He later got into a great deal of trouble, losing his job at Google thanks to some of his comments in the book. He's now a member of the cancelled elite.
